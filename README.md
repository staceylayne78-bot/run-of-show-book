# Run-of-Show Book

Ten workshops written out minute by minute, plus the method for assembling your own.

**Read it:** [staceylayne78.github.io/run-of-show-book](https://staceylayne78.github.io/run-of-show-book/)

---

A design thinking toolkit tells you which tool to pick. It doesn't tell you how to
run the room — what to say, how long each thing takes, when to vote, who decides.
Most enterprise design groups have a tool library: forty-odd canvases, each with
prompts and a stated output, and nothing anywhere about the minute-to-minute.

This is that missing layer. Ten complete run-of-shows, every step timed, with the
words to say, the handoff to the next step written out, and the source credited.

| # | Session | Time |
|---|---------|------|
| 1 | Blueprint the Service | 4h |
| 2 | Frame the Challenge | 2h 15m |
| 3 | Ideas to Concepts | 3h |
| 4 | Sequence the Work | 2h |
| 5 | Strategy Signal | 2 sessions |
| 6 | One Owner, One Afternoon | 1h 30m |
| 7 | Lightning Decision Jam | 1h |
| 8 | What Do We Need to Know | 1h 15m |
| 9 | Requirements and Who Decides | 3h |
| 10 | What Will Kill This | 45m |

Plus: a readiness gate, what to set expectations about, how the Decider model bends
when the room isn't near-peers, altitude on the wall, a handoff audit for sessions
that don't flow, a seven-module training plan, and the method for composing a
run-of-show nobody has written yet.

## Where it comes from

The tools are standard design thinking. The facilitation layer is not, and that is
the part that took reading:

- **AJ&Smart / Facilitator.com** — the Decider role, "work together alone," the heat
  map / straw poll / Decider vote sequence, the twenty-second rule, What·Why·How.
- **This Is Service Design Doing** (Stickdorn, Lawrence, Hormess, Schneider) —
  journey map and blueprint depth, co-creation, the facilitation theory underneath.
  [54 free method pages](https://www.thisisservicedesigndoing.com/methods).
- **Gary Klein** — the pre-mortem, from *Performing a Project Premortem*, Harvard
  Business Review, September 2007.
- **Liberating Structures** (Lipmanowicz & McCandless) — TRIZ, 1-2-4-All, 25/10.
  [All 33, free](https://www.liberatingstructures.com/).
- **Workshopr.io** — the commitment round, and
  [12 more scripted sessions](https://www.workshopr.io/library/recipes/).

Steps that use something outside a standard toolkit are tagged **outside** in the
run-of-show. Steps that are mine and have no source at all are tagged
**not from a source**. Per-session credit sits at the foot of every run-of-show.

## Running it

One file, no build, no dependencies. Open `index.html`, or serve the folder:

```
python3 -m http.server 8000
```
